--> Required Preprocessing Steps:-

1. Lowercasing

Convert all text to lowercase
 “OMG” → “omg”

2. Remove Emojis

Remove or convert emojis into words
 😂 → “laugh”

3. Expand Slang

Convert short forms into full words
 “u” → “you”

4. Spell Correction

Fix typos
 “beleive” → “believe”

5. Remove Special Characters

Remove symbols like !, ?, etc.

6. Remove Repeated Letters

 “soooo” → “so”

7. Tokenization

Split sentence into words
 “i love ml” → [i, love, ml]
