--> What is Prompt Engineering?

Prompt Engineering is the process of designing clear and specific inputs (prompts) to get accurate and useful responses from an AI model.

--> Examples of Weak vs Strong Prompts:-

1. Resume Generation:-

Weak Prompt:
 “Make my resume”

Strong Prompt:
 “Create a professional resume for a Computer Science student skilled in Python, Machine Learning, and Web Development. Include sections for education, projects, skills, and internships. Keep it concise and ATS-friendly.”

Comparison:

Weak → Too vague
Strong → Clear role, skills, format


2. Business Idea:-

Weak Prompt:
 “Give me a business idea”

Strong Prompt:
 “Suggest a low-budget tech startup idea for college students in India, including target audience, problem solved, and revenue model.”

Comparison:

Weak → No direction
Strong → Specific audience + constraints


3. Study Plan:-

Weak Prompt:
👉 “Make a study plan”

Strong Prompt:
👉 “Create a 7-day study plan for learning Machine Learning basics, including daily topics, practice tasks, and revision time for a beginner.”

Comparison:

Weak → Generic
Strong → Time-bound + structured
