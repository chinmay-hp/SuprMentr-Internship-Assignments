1. What is Semantic Similarity?

Semantic similarity refers to how closely two words are related in meaning.

High similarity → similar meaning (e.g., happy & joyful)
Low similarity → different meaning (e.g., happy & sad)


2. Word Pairs with Explanation:-

1. Happy – Joyful
Similarity: High
Explanation: Both words express a feeling of happiness and positivity.

2. Big – Large
Similarity: High
Explanation: Both describe something of great size or magnitude.

3. Car – Vehicle
Similarity: Medium
Explanation: A car is a type of vehicle, so they are related but not identical.

4. Teacher – Student
Similarity: Low to Medium
Explanation: They are related in context (education) but have different roles.

5. Hot – Cold
Similarity: Opposite (Antonyms)
Explanation: These words have completely opposite meanings.
