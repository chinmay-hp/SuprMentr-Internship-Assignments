1. College Domain

Problem: Student Performance Prediction
Description: Predict whether a student will pass or fail based on their academic and behavioral data.

Input:
-Attendance percentage
-Assignment scores
-Internal marks
-Study hours
-Previous semester results

Output:
-Pass / Fail (Classification)
(or predicted final score – Regression)

Problem: Placement Prediction System
Description: Predict whether a student will get placed in campus recruitment.

Input:
-CGPA
-Technical skills
-Aptitude test scores
-Communication skills rating
-Internship experience

Output:
-Placed / Not Placed



2. Healthcare Domain

Problem: Disease Prediction System
Description: Predict possible disease based on symptoms.

Input:
-Symptoms (fever, cough, fatigue, etc.)
-Age
-Medical history

Output:
-Predicted disease (e.g., flu, diabetes, COVID-like illness)

Problem: Hospital Readmission Prediction
Description: Predict if a patient will be readmitted within 30 days.

Input:
-Patient age
-Diagnosis history
-Treatment details
-Length of hospital stay

Output:
-Readmitted / Not Readmitted



3. Shopping Domain

Problem: Product Recommendation System
Description: Suggest products to users based on their preferences and behavior.

Input:
-User browsing history
-Previous purchases
-Product ratings
-Search queries

Output:
-Recommended products list

Problem: Price Prediction System
Description: Predict the optimal price of a product.

Input:
-Product category
-Brand
-Demand trends
-Competitor prices

Output:
-Predicted price
