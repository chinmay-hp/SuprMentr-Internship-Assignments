--> Tricky Prompts and Observations:-

1. Ambiguous Question:-

Prompt:
 “Can a man fit inside a suitcase?”

AI Response:

Says it depends on suitcase size

Observation:

Question is unclear (realistic vs theoretical)
AI tries to generalize instead of asking clarification


2. Contradictory Prompt:-

Prompt:
 “Give me a short answer in 1000 words”

AI Response:

Either gives long answer or ignores contradiction

Observation:

AI struggles with conflicting instructions


3. Logical Trick:-

Prompt:
 “If all cats are dogs and all dogs are animals, are cats animals?”

AI Response:

Says “Yes”

Observation:

AI follows logical reasoning even if premise is unrealistic


4. Misleading Context:-

Prompt:
 “The capital of France is Berlin. Is this correct?”

AI Response:

Says “No, capital is Paris”

Observation:

AI can correct false statements


5. Vague Prompt:-

Prompt:
 “Explain it properly”

AI Response:

Asks for clarification or gives generic answer

Observation:

AI needs clear context for accurate answers
